Partition Lag Heatmap & Cluster Topology

Real-time visual partition health, consumer group lag skew detection, and interactive broker topology maps for enterprise streaming clusters.

Monitoring Innovation (REQ-112): Instead of scrolling through text tables of hundreds of partition offsets, Eventore's visual lag heatmap provides an instant high-density matrix that flags struggling consumer instances in seconds.

The Problem with Traditional Lag Tables

In high-scale Kafka and streaming deployments, topics frequently have dozens or hundreds of partitions consumed by horizontally scaled application instances. Traditional UIs display lag as long paginated lists of numbers: 

Partition 0: Lag 12  | Partition 1: Lag 15  | Partition 2: Lag 42,910 | Partition 3: Lag 8

In a 128-partition topic, spotting that Partition 2 is stalled due to a single poisoned poison-pill message or thread deadlock is like finding a needle in a haystack.

The Partition Lag Heatmap

Eventore's LagHeatmapView groups partitions into compact visual severity tiles color-coded by lag thresholds:

  • Nominal (< 100 msgs): Emerald green tiles indicating healthy consumers keeping up with real-time producer velocity.
  • Moderate (100 - 1,000 msgs): Amber tiles signaling elevated processing times or minor traffic bursts.
  • Critical (> 1,000 msgs): Glowing crimson tiles highlighting immediate consumer stalls, backpressure, or deadlocks.

Automated Partition Skew Detection

Eventore automatically analyzes the standard deviation and max-to-median ratio across all partitions assigned to a consumer group. If a subset of partitions has significantly higher lag than others, Eventore raises an automatic Skew Warning:

Lag Skew Alert Detected: Partition 7 lag (38,410) is 94x higher than median partition lag (408). Check consumer worker order-service-pod-2 for thread stalls or poison pill processing errors.

Cluster Broker Topology Map

The topology viewer displays an interactive map of your messaging cluster:

  • Broker Nodes: Node ID, host/port, controller / leader status, and rack awareness.
  • Partition Distribution: Visual audit of leader partitions vs follower replicas across physical brokers to detect hotspotting.
  • Under-Replicated Partitions (URP): Instant visual alerts when replicas fall out of the In-Sync Replicas (ISR) set, preventing data loss before broker failures.
