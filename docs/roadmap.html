Where EventOre goes next.

These are backlog directions, not release commitments. No target versions or delivery dates have been assigned here. Items marked deferred or future design are not currently shipping capabilities.

Upcoming directions

Planned

Schema Registry: Confluent & AWS Glue integration

Native wire-format deserialization for Confluent Schema Registry and AWS Glue Schema Registry across Kafka and Pulsar streams.

Proposed

Per-connection rate limiting

Bound publish, search, and subscription traffic per connection. Payload-size and concurrent-subscription limits already exist; request-rate controls are a separate backlog item.

Deeper MQTT and JMS inspection

Explore richer queue and retained-message inspection while respecting each broker’s semantics and avoiding destructive reads.

Live cloud-broker CI coverage

Already introduced

Enterprise identity (OIDC/JWT), multi-tenant workspaces, distributed subscription fan-out (Redis & Local), autonomous incident triage MCP tooling, schema-aware publishing, DLQ redrive, message replay, masking, tracing, lag visualization, external stores, and bridging already have development implementations. They belong in the feature history, not a list of future promises.
